## ⲧⲉⲛϩⲏⲧ (Overview)

**ⲡⲓⲥⲁϧⲟ** (*Pi-Saho*, Coptic for *The Teacher* or *The Scholar*) is an open-source translator that converts English text into Coptic script. Designed for students, researchers, and enthusiasts, ⲡⲓⲥⲁϧⲟ bridges modern natural language processing with ancient Coptic linguistics, supporting dialect-aware translations, grammatical accuracy, and transliteration.

---

## ⲛⲓⲁⲣⲉⲧⲏ (Features)

- 🔤 **English to Coptic Translation:** Translate words, common phrases, and full sentences into authentic Coptic script.
- 🏛️ **Dialect Support:** Switch target translations between **Bohairic** (ⲃⲱϩⲁⲓⲣⲓⲁ) and **Sahidic** (ⲥⲁϩⲓⲇⲓⲕ) dialects.
- 🗣️ **Phonetic Transliteration:** Generates Latin phonetics alongside Coptic script to assist with pronunciation.
- 📖 **Lexicon & Grammar Matching:** Handles Coptic sentence structures, verb conjugations, and Greek loanwords accurately.
- ⌨️ **Coptic Keyboard & Output:** Clean Unicode output compatible with standard Coptic fonts and keyboards.
